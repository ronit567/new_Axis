Axis is a buy-and-sell marketplace for university students. Every account is tied to a verified university email address, so you know the person you are messaging belongs to your university community.

What you can do

- Browse listings by category: textbooks, furniture, electronics, tickets, clothing, sports and more
- Search with filters for category, condition and maximum price
- Post a listing with photos and a price, or mark it as a giveaway or open to trades
- Message sellers inside the app and make an offer
- Save listings to come back to later
- Keep one conversation per listing, and delete any you no longer need
- Get in-app notifications when someone messages you or saves your listing
- See a seller's program, year and other listings before you reply

Built to be safe

- Report any listing, conversation or profile. Every report is reviewed.
- Block anyone. Blocking hides you from each other across the feed, search, profiles and messages.
- Listings, profiles and messages are filtered for objectionable text.
- Delete your account and your data at any time from Settings.

How it works

Axis does not process payments. Buyers and sellers agree on a price in chat and exchange items in person.
